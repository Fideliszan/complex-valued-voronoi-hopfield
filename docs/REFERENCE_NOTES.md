# Reference placement and bibliographic corrections

The introduction attaches references to the relevant application, algebra, or update rule. Citations sharing a narrow claim remain grouped. The bibliography includes cited works only.

Application examples are restricted to what the sources describe: wave processing, interferometric radar, coherent optical computing, communication constellations, and gray-level associative memory. Gray-level image studies are not presented as evidence for color-image applications.

Primary records used for metadata corrections:

- [Hirose publication list](https://www.eis.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/plist.html): the merits-and-origins paper is an IJCNN 2009 contribution.
- [Optical computing article](https://doi.org/10.1016/1069-0115(94)90014-0): phase-sensitive coherent optical computing.
- [Radar article](https://globals.ieice.org/en_transactions/electronics/10.1587/e84-c_12_1932/_p): Hirose and Minami, 2001.
- [Aizenberg monograph](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-20353-4): sole author Igor Aizenberg, 2011.
- [Tanaka laboratory publication list](https://dyn.web.nitech.ac.jp/en/publications): Tanaka and Aihara, IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks 20(9), 1463–1473, 2009.
- [Kobayashi publication list](https://hdnnlab.com/publication-list-model/): publication years, venues, volumes, and pages for the individual network families.
- [Valle publication list](https://www.ime.unicamp.br/~valle/journals.php): the recurrent correlation paper appears in Neurocomputing 432, 111–123, 2021.

Bibliography keys are internal identifiers; some retain older year suffixes for continuity. The year displayed in the bibliography is the corrected publication year. These editorial corrections do not change the experimental protocols, measurements, or convergence statements.
